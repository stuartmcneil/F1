2026 has 23 rounds after the Bahrain and Saudi Arabian rounds were cancelled (Sepang hosts a replacement in October). The planner assigns each unused chip to the remaining weekend where it is likely to pay off most — one chip per weekend. Override any pick and the rest re-plan around it.

Chip plan

Rules of thumb: 3x Boost on a Sprint weekend (three scoring sessions tripled); Limitless where the expensive cars are safest bets; No Negative at the crash-prone street races; Wildcard when the form-book shifts or before a run of flyaways; Autopilot where you can't call the winner; Final Fix to react to a wet or penalty-hit qualifying.
